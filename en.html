Privacy Policy

AngleWings is a single-player flight game with offline core gameplay. No registration or sign-in is required. This policy explains data handling by the core game, development test ads, and this website.

1. Scope

This policy covers the core game, development builds with optional test ads, support emails, and the privacy policy and technical support pages on this website.

2. Data handled by the game

The core game does not upload game saves to the developer. It has no remote accounts, cloud saves, chat, or online leaderboards. Development builds include optional Google AdMob rewarded test ads; see Section 3 for advertising data practices. Ad viewing is not currently enabled in Release builds.

Coins, experience, level and score records, weapon levels, ship and companion ownership and equipment, and random ad presentation IDs used to prevent duplicate rewards are stored in the app's sandbox to restore local progress and reconcile rewards. These random IDs are not device advertising identifiers and are not uploaded by the app. The app may keep a previous valid save and local copies of damaged saves for recovery. The app does not upload these files.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising identifier permissions. There are currently no real-money purchases or in-app purchases. Coins are earned through gameplay; development builds also offer an optional test ad for 100 coins. Test ads do not generate real advertising revenue.

3. Sharing and platform services

We do not sell or rent game saves or upload coins, equipment, or progress to advertising providers. Development builds include the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its bundled User Messaging Platform (UMP) component. The current adapter initializes and requests Google test ads only when you choose to watch.

The Google advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and video interactions, and crash, performance, and diagnostic information for advertising, security, measurement, and analytics. Test requests disable personalized publisher privacy treatment and publisher first-party IDs and do not request ATT tracking permission. This does not mean the SDK processes no data. See Google SDK data disclosures and the Google Privacy Policy.

Watching ads is optional. You can skip ad rewards and continue offline gameplay. Google's retention periods and privacy request channels are governed by its privacy policy; use Google's privacy channels to manage or request deletion of data it processes. Commercial ads are not enabled; consent flows and disclosures will be reviewed before any future activation.

Apple's operating system, App Store, device diagnostics, and backup services may process data under their own policies and your settings. Whether app data is included in a device or cloud backup depends on your device and backup settings. AngleWings itself does not provide cross-device synchronization.

4. Emails you choose to send

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use this information only to reply to, investigate, and resolve your support or privacy request, not for advertising. Sending email is not required to play the game.

Messages are processed through your chosen email service and our receiving email service under their respective policies. Do not send passwords, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive information. The app does not automatically send emails, saves, or diagnostic data.

We retain correspondence only as needed to handle your request and necessary follow-up, or to retain required information where legally obligated. You may use the contact email below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided, or to withdraw consent for further voluntary provision of information. We will handle requests after reasonable verification and in accordance with applicable requirements.

5. Retaining and deleting local saves

Local saves remain on your device so you can continue playing. Choosing “Delete App” in system settings deletes the app's local saves. “Offload App” may retain documents and data. Copies in device or cloud backups must be managed through the relevant system or backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore earlier game data.

The developer holds no remote game accounts or saves. There is therefore no remote account deletion process, and we cannot read, restore, or delete game progress on your device for you.

6. Children's privacy

The core game does not ask users for names, ages, or contact details. See Section 3 for advertising SDK data practices. Children taking part in development testing or requesting email support should ask a parent or guardian for help and avoid providing unnecessary personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was inappropriately provided to us may contact us to request its deletion.

7. Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation for details.

We have added no advertisements, analytics scripts, trackers, or forms to this website. It does not receive or upload your game saves. Visiting this website requires an internet connection; offline gameplay does not depend on visiting it.

8. Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date, provide notices, obtain necessary consent, and update store privacy disclosures as applicable. Future versions will be covered by the corresponding policy.
