1. Getting started

  1. Tap “开始飞行” on the home screen. Drag horizontally in the lower part of the battlefield to start flying and control your ship.
  2. Your ship and equipped companions fire automatically. Tap the skill button in the lower-right corner to use your active skill.
  3. Tap the upper-right pause button, then choose “继续飞行” to resume or “结束并结算” to end the run and save the results. Skill and power-up timers stop while paused.
  4. Use “航舰与升级” to preview, unlock, and select ships or upgrade weapons. Use “地图与伙伴” to choose a map, unlock companions, and equip the left and right companion slots.

2. Do coins cost real money?

No. There are no real-money purchases or in-app purchases. Coins are earned through gameplay and saved locally when a run is settled. Use them to unlock ships and companions or upgrade weapons. Both Debug and Release builds offer optional AdMob ad rewards. The current ads are clearly labeled test ads; commercial ads and real revenue are not enabled. Tap “看广告领金币” on the home screen and complete the reward requirements to receive 100 coins. Early dismissal or loading failure earns no coins; a failed save can be retried without another ad. You can continue offline gameplay without watching ads.

A new save starts with 0 coins. Keep playing to earn enough coins for an unlock or upgrade.

Ads may have no fill, fail to connect, or time out. Cancel loading to return to the game. If the rewards page offers ad privacy options, use them to manage your choices. To report inappropriate or age-inappropriate ads, select “举报广告” on that page or email liuzhengli0706@163.com with the time and content. You may choose to attach a screenshot with personal information hidden. You must confirm sending the email.

3. Where is my progress saved?

Coins, experience, equipment, and records are stored in the app's sandbox on your current device. No registration or sign-in is needed. There are no cloud saves or cross-device synchronization. Progress is saved locally when a run ends and its results are settled.

Choosing “Delete App” in system settings deletes local saves. “Offload App” may retain documents and data. Manage device or cloud backups through the relevant system settings. The developer has no remote save and cannot restore deleted local progress for you.

If a save cannot be read, record the complete message and contact support. To avoid losing progress, do not delete and reinstall the app as your first troubleshooting step.

4. Audio and pause troubleshooting

The sound button in the lower-right area of the home screen switches game audio on or off. If it shows muted audio, tap it to enable sound. Check your device's media volume and Bluetooth or headphone output.

If the game is paused, select “继续飞行” in the pause menu. If display or touch problems continue, finish and settle the run before closing and reopening the app. Contact us if the problem persists.

5. Reporting an issue or suggesting a feature

Email issues, general feedback, or feature requests to liuzhengli0706@163.com. For troubleshooting, please consider including:

  • Your device model, iOS / iPadOS version, and app version.
  • The affected screen, steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results.
  • Whether the issue repeats, and any error message or screenshot you choose to provide.

Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive information. The support feature does not automatically send emails or attach saves or diagnostic data. Advertising SDK data practices are described in the privacy policy. We handle requests using the information you provide; live chat support is not currently available.
